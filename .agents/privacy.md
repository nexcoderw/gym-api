# Privacy

## Standards

Minimize personal data, document purpose/retention, enforce tenant/resource access, encrypt in transit
and at rest, avoid wide responses, support authorized correction/deletion/export workflows, and govern
backups/logs/integrations. Rwanda privacy and residency obligations require legal validation before
production; do not claim approval.

## Forbidden practices

No real identities in tests/docs, secrets or personal data in URLs/logs, indefinite retention, broad
staff visibility, unmanaged exports, or analytics reuse without governance.

## Example and checklist

Use `member@example.com` and placeholder phone numbers. Review necessity, consent/legal basis,
residency, retention, deletion, audit, backup, provider sharing, and incident impact.
